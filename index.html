1. How to Request Account Deletion

Users of the Kaison Marketing Company Registration App can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. Follow the steps below to submit a deletion request:

  1. Send an email to our data protection and support team using the button or email address provided below.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - Kaison Marketing App.
  3. Include the full name and registered email address or phone number associated with your app account so we can verify your identity.
  4. Specify whether you want to delete your entire account and all associated data, or only specific business registration records while keeping your login active.
